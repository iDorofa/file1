REMOVE WEAPON GRIP


PICK THE CORRECT FILES IF USED WITH DECALS!
At the beginning of the game, the ripper doc Viktor will install the first Weapon Grip cyberware for your character. Weapon Grips include the Ballistic Coprocessor and Smart Link. Decals replace the meshes of the Weapon Grip, so make sure that you pick the correct files if you want to hide a part of the Weapon Grip and use Decals on only one arm.

WEAPON GRIP CONSISTS OF TWO MESHES!
There are a couple of images showing this in the images folder. It has metal bits, and if replaced with Decals they add them to the left arm. It also has a palm texture, which is used by the right arm Decals. That means if you are using Decals for the Left arm, you can add the Remove Weapon Grip mod from the "Palm texture (R)" folder.


FILES INCLUDED IN THIS FOLDER:

aa_Remove_WeaponGrip -- Completely removes the Weapon Grip for male and female characters.

aa_Remove_WeaponGrip_F -- Completely removes the Weapon Grip for female characters only.
aa_Remove_WeaponGrip_M -- Completely removes the Weapon Grip for male characters only.

aa_Remove_WeaponGrip_F_L -- Removes the metal bits from the Weapon Grip. Can be used with right arm Decals. For female characters.
aa_Remove_WeaponGrip_F_R -- Removes the palm texture from the Weapon Grip. Can be used with left arm Decals. For female characters.
aa_Remove_WeaponGrip_M_L -- Removes the metal bits from the Weapon Grip. Can be used with right arm Decals. For male characters.
aa_Remove_WeaponGrip_M_R -- Removes the palm texture from the Weapon Grip. Can be used with left arm Decals. For male characters.


The files should be placed in the "Cyberpunk 2077\archive\pc\mod" folder. Only place the .archive files there, not the folders.

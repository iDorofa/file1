BLADE LIBRARY


PICK THE CORRECT OPEN OR CLOSED ARM BLADE!
Most modded Mantis arms do not open up when in use. They require blades for closed arms. Arasaka Mantis Remade arms will open up when used and can not use blades intended for closed arms. Pick the correct blades from the Open or Closed folders, depending on what arms you install. All blades are divided between male and female, left and right arms.

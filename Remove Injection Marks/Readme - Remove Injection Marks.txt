REMOVE INJECTION MARKS


When your character meets Viktor and he installs their first cyberware, he injects their right arm with a local anesthesia. The mark from the injection will stay on the arm through the whole game. On some cyberarms, like the Arasaka Thin arms, the mark will be visible on top of the arm.


FILES INCLUDED IN THIS FOLDER:

aa_Remove_InjectionMarks -- Removes injection marks from both male and female characters.

aa_Remove_InjectionMarks_F -- Removes injection marks from female characters only.
aa_Remove_InjectionMarks_M -- Removes injection marks from male characters only.


The files should be placed in the "Cyberpunk 2077\archive\pc\mod" folder. Only place the .archive files there, not the folders.
